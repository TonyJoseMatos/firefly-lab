# Hybrid Notes: Firefly ↔ ComfyUI / Unreal

Practical crossovers for moving assets between Adobe Firefly and existing Gigadream pipelines.

## Firefly → ComfyUI
- Generate base lifestyle or environment plates in Firefly with strong style lock.  
- Bring into ComfyUI for further consistency passes, ControlNet refinement, or multi-model fusion.  
- Use Firefly outputs as style references or locked masters.

## Firefly → Unreal
- Generate high-detail environment and material reference plates.  
- Use as source for texture and lighting studies inside Unreal.  
- Maintain cinematic continuity between Firefly stills and real-time scenes.

## ElevenLabs Crossover (Audio)
- Pair Firefly visual sequences with ElevenLabs voice or sound design for motion and presentation assets.  
- Keep brand tone consistent across visual and audio layers.

These hybrids treat Firefly as one node in a larger creative operating system.
